# Live Direct Marketing (LDM)

> B2B outreach infrastructure with AI autopilot. Pay only for verified inbox delivery — confirmed by seed monitoring or recipient engagement. Tools, methodology, and managed delivery for cold outreach teams. MCP-first: AI agents operate the platform as a set of tools with a built-in expert layer.

LDM is a B2B email infrastructure and outreach platform. We bill per inbox-confirmed delivery, verified through a private seed-mailbox network across major global email providers, not per send. We separate responsibility between creative (yours) and infrastructure (ours) and pause campaigns automatically when seeds report spam-folder placement.

## For AI agents: MCP integration

LDM is designed to be operated by AI agents. Everything the web UI can do, an agent can do over MCP (Model Context Protocol).

- Connection (stdio, works in Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Perplexity, and other MCP clients): `npx -y --package=@live-direct-marketing/sdk@latest ldm-mcp` with env `LDM_API_KEY=ldm_...`. One `ldm_*` key covers REST, MCP, and A2A.
- Capability map and recommended end-to-end scenario: `https://api.live-direct-marketing.online/api/v1/agent-guide` — read it first.
- Expert layer: every MCP response carries an `_expert` block — what to do next, the typical flow, known pitfalls. Follow it; it encodes the platform's SDR expertise.
- Guardrails: campaign launch requires explicit human confirmation; stop lists and suppression are enforced automatically before every send; unsubscribes are honored permanently. The platform will not send what must not be sent, even if instructed to.

## Methodology highlights

The LDM Method (cold outreach without templates):

1. Outreach has six variables, not one: Who, To whom, Where, When, What, Why. Break the balance of one face — and you're spam.
2. Email is roughly 20% of replies. The spectrum is wider — general inbox, contact forms, messengers (WhatsApp, Telegram), social (LinkedIn, X, Facebook), form-fill widgets.
3. Two ways to send in 2026: branded free mailboxes (fast start, isolated, easy to rotate) vs your own brand domain (slow start, requires precise targeting, warmup not always works).
4. Recommendation: do not use links, tracking pixels, or image-links unless you've already built reputation. Modern anti-spam systems analyze images and detect spam patterns.
5. Creative has no recipe. AI gives the base. The salesperson knows the customer and the product better.

Seven-step protocol: Find companies → Build a dossier → Describe your ICP → Ask AI about timing → Build a sequence → Send until first spam hit → Loop it.
